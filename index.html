Your files

Your PDFs are read inside this browser and never uploaded anywhere. What the planner pulls out of them is saved in this browser so you don't have to upload again.

    Start with the course schedule

    1. Course schedule (required): the full interim course schedule PDF for the semester you're planning.
    2. Curriculum plan (optional): adds course names, credit units, prerequisites, and which SUSS Core courses you still need.
    3. Study plan (optional): adds the courses recommended for your intake and semester, so they're added for you.
    4. Overseas course info sheet (optional, e.g. NIE351): adds trip destinations, costs and dates, and checks them for clashes.
